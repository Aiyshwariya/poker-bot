# Strategy

The logic is in `strategy.py`. `agent.py` calls into it and falls back to CHECK_CALL on any error.

**Preflop.** `preflop_table.json` stores, for each starting hand, its equity vs a random hand,
vs the top 40% and vs the top 15% of hands, plus its percentile. It was generated by
`tools/build_preflop.py`. When facing a raise, the opponent's range is estimated from how
often they raise preflop.

**Postflop.** Monte Carlo over runouts and opponent hands until a per-decision deadline.
Each opponent bet or raise narrows their range to a stronger slice of hands, scaled by how
often that opponent raises. We call when equity beats the pot odds, bet or raise for value
against the range that would call, and bluff only against opponents who fold often.

**Time.** About 3 s of compute per half, out of the 8 s bank.

**Testing.** `tools/sim.py` runs a fast duplicate match in-process. `tools/tourney.py` runs
many matches in parallel against the baselines and the bots in `tools/refbots/`.
